Privacy Policy for Claro

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Claro ("the App") is a loan and mortgage calculator app. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data We Collect

All loan data you enter is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Notifications

If you enable payment reminders, the App uses local notifications. No notification data is sent to external servers.

Permissions

  • Notifications: For optional payment reminders.
  • Internet: Required for loading ads.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
